# Result figures

## Annual median NDVI

`ndvi_annual_median_2020.png` shows the spatial distribution
of annual median NDVI within the study area.

Each pixel represents the median of its valid 2020 observations.
The map is not a single-date image.

The numerical raster is available in `../exports/`.

## Monthly NDVI variation

`ndvi_monthly_timeseries_2020.png` shows the regional mean
of each monthly median NDVI composite.

All 12 months have valid regional results.

- Highest monthly value: July, 0.7926
- Lowest monthly value: December, 0.0917
- Difference between highest and lowest monthly values: 0.7010

The series shows pronounced seasonal variation, with high
NDVI in July and August and lower values in winter.

The statistics include all retained land-cover types.
Differences in valid observation coverage between months
may affect the regional means.
